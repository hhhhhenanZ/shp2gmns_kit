\section{Master lookup: free-flow time, capacity, VDF}

\textbf{Free-flow time \C{@ftime}} \src{[FT]}: \C{@speed} is rounded \textbf{up} to a bin, then
$\C{@ftime} = \C{length}\,/\,\text{bin speed}\times60$. The converter uses \C{@ftime} as given; recomputed
from \C{@speed} it matches on 100\% of VDF 1--6, 8 links \src{[m]}.

\textbf{What \C{ref_time} / \C{timau} is} \src{[G], [VDF]}\\[2pt]
CMAP runs a \emph{static} equilibrium assignment per period. Every link ends with one volume (\C{DATA1}, whole 2 h)
and the VDF time at that volume. One time for 4--6 pm at the period-average flow rate: no change within the period,
no queue spillback. A model output (``raw model results'' \src{[G]}). 2{,}823 links carry zero volume and keep free-flow time.
\end{minipage}
